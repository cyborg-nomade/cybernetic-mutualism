# Blog prose style

This guide applies only to prose intended for publication on the blog,
including files in `posts/`. It does not govern research designs, case notes,
evidence records, models, technical documentation, the roadmap, or other
scientific and repository prose. Those materials use conventional
capitalization and whatever structures best fit their purpose.

Source-faithful quotations, code, identifiers, bibliographic titles, and proper
names retain their required forms.

## Persona and tone

The blog voice is cynical, ironic, and comfortable with sarcasm. Irony should
put pressure on a claim, institution, political piety, or the author's own
former certainty; it should not merely decorate a paragraph with jokes. Let the
reader feel that the argument knows how absurd its objects can be. Keep the
literal claim recoverable beneath the irony so that tone does not become an
escape from accuracy.

Poetic and abstract horror belongs to the voice. Impersonal systems reproduce,
feedback escapes its designers, familiar institutions become alien machinery,
and apparently reasonable local actions can assemble something no participant
intended. Use images of darkness, appetite, recursion, possession, machinery,
and hostile scale when they disclose the structure of the argument. Avoid
empty cosmic language that could be removed without changing the thought.

Nick Land is a major stylistic and theoretical influence. The authorial
self-description to keep in view is **a mutualist who read too much Land**.
The prose may carry Landian velocity, black humor, impersonal process, positive
feedback, runaway intelligence, temporal pressure, and the sense that systems
think through their participants. Do not sanitize that inheritance or pretend
the resemblance is accidental.

The political divergence must remain equally visible. Cybernetic mutualism is
far to Land's left: description is not endorsement, capital is not owed victory,
inhuman dynamics do not acquire moral authority through power or persistence,
and emancipation cannot be delegated to acceleration. Treat the influence as a
productive contamination rather than allegiance. The tension should energize
the prose instead of being resolved into either imitation or ritual denunciation.

Sarcasm and horror must never alter quotations, overstate evidence, conceal a
model limitation, or turn uncertainty into theatrical certainty. The persona
can be vicious toward abstractions and the systems that demand reverence. It
should not be casually cruel toward vulnerable people or substitute sneering
for analysis.

## Links and references

Link generously. Primary texts, papers, archives, datasets, documentation,
other blogs, old posts, and productive disagreements may all become branches
from the essay. Place a link where an interested reader would naturally want
to leave the main path and inspect the source or tangent.

Links need not all function as formal evidence. Make the prose distinguish a
source supporting a factual claim from an influence, analogy, provocation, or
neighboring argument. Prefer descriptive link text over bare URLs, and avoid a
detached bibliography when inline links can reveal the relation more clearly.

## Capitalization

Ordinary sentences begin with lowercase letters, including sentences following
periods and other terminal punctuation. Capitals belong only where the term
itself requires them, such as proper names, acronyms, and initialisms. The
first-person pronoun is lowercase *i*.

## Structure

Do not use tables in blog prose. Rewrite comparisons and mappings as connected
paragraphs.

Avoid topic lists unless sequence, procedure, or another genuinely list-shaped
relationship would become less clear in prose. Do not use lists merely to
inventory the themes of an argument.

## Spelling

Prefer American English spelling, including forms such as *organization*,
*centralization*, *behavior*, *labor*, *modeled*, and *totalization*.
