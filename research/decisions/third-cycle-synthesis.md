## Findings That Change the Programme

### Archive access and mechanism observability are different requirements

All four project development lists and the board have 36 of 36 primary months
covered; G1 requires 33. The additional special-meeting inventory also passes.
This supports the feasibility of the registered source retrieval and cohort.
It does not supply a public receiving act, establish its authorized actor or
locate its date relative to the sender. G2 tests those additional observations.

| Horizon | A → C classifiable families | C → A classifiable families | Reached decision |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 90 days, primary | 0/34 (0%) | 1/41 (2.44%) | G2 stop |
| 60 days | 0/34 (0%) | 1/41 (2.44%) | G2 stop |
| 180 days | 0/34 (0%) | 1/41 (2.44%) | G2 stop |

Both directions meet the four-family and two-PMC minima and span all four
PMCs. Both fail the 75% joint classifiability requirement. A shared opportunity
family is classifiable only when every recorded eligible project response
meets the frozen conjunction. Unknown responses remain in the denominator.
The 136 C → A project records at each horizon represent 41 families, not 136
independent opportunities. A visible response by one project cannot establish
the other responses in that family.

The [family observability table](../data/asf-item4-audit-2026-11-10/outputs/family-observability.csv)
and [field missingness table](../data/asf-item4-audit-2026-11-10/outputs/field-missingness.csv)
identify the failure directly. At 90 days, five A → C records have known
receivers, yet all 34 have unknown temporal order. Four C → A records have
known receivers; only two have dated within-horizon sequences, and shared-family
requirements leave one classifiable family. Missing receiving conduct, undated
acts and uncertain historical publication remain distinct recorded limitations.
The unchanged G2 result at 180 days shows that the registered horizon extension
does not solve this run's observability problem. It does not rule out a better
measurement design or a different future corpus.

### Reproducibility does not establish coding stability or causal identification

The frozen integrity check found zero errors across the 14 native tables,
including 2,946 measurement rows and 4,740 source records. Exact reconstruction
reproduces the locked source/native byte map and blank bundle. Those results
support reproducibility of the submitted records and implementation. They do
not independently verify source interpretation, completeness declarations or
outside-chat abstention.

Because G2 fails, G3 was not reached. There is no initial recode, agreement
score, disagreement set or reconciliation to assess. Coding stability is
unmeasured; neither passing computational checks nor common unknowns supply
a stability result. The output explicitly retains absent G3 pass/score values.
No independent second researcher was simulated and no recode was retried.

The reciprocal/one-way/uncoupled/common-shock comparison, signed witness
summaries and nested mixed-effect tests remain unreached. The result therefore
selects none of those accounts. It also differs from the registered
indeterminate causal category, which would require all gates to pass before
that adjudication. No removal intervention was conducted; the necessity of
either pole remains untested.

### The corpus has reusable descriptive value with a narrower empirical mapping

The completed frame contains 368 analysis family/project pairs, including the
36-release baseline, plus 123 audit-only pairs. Both directions at all three
horizons give 2,946 measurement rows. The 979-pair eligible screening universe
includes unsampled releases; it does not imply that required coding is missing.
Audit-only supplements remain outside the analysis denominator.

The four registered reusable products are published: month-level access;
screening/family inventories and the fixed baseline log; direction × recorded
sign × authority-shift × disposition tables; and per-record/family blockers.
They retain identifiers that connect to source bytes, dates, locators, contrasts
and rival explanations in the accepted package. Recorded first-pass labels
remain descriptive, without a passed coding-stability or causal gate.

The prespecified consequence for CM-04 is to narrow this design's empirical
mapping because G1 passes and G2 fails through unclassifiable opportunities.
This limits the demonstrated usefulness of the A/C vocabulary for this public
record design. It does not establish that institutional autonomy and
coordination are unreal or that no future operationalization could discriminate
an account. The earlier formal-model narrowing remains in force: this study
validates no numerical parameter, attractor, four-regime generality or predictive
advantage. Normative legitimacy receives no inference from a governance label
or a model's performance.

## Ordered Handoff and Next Discriminator

Prioritize a small prospective observability pilot before another full causal
comparison in this domain. The decision-relevant question is whether a new,
predeclared intake and source-capture design can establish independently dated
sender acts and receiving conduct for the full eligible receiver set. The
accepted missingness records motivate this proposal; they do not demonstrate
that prospective capture will succeed or identify which new source will work.
No fourth cycle is activated here.

| Proposed step | Task type | Reviewable deliverable and decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Specify an observability pilot before new evidence | Writing/research design | A separate preregistration defining a new future intake window, opportunity-family/receiver universe, public-source rules, date/role/publication requirements, budget and explicit feasibility/stop criteria. This is the next proposed item. |
| 2. Prepare any required capture/validation tools | Code, conditional | A separate implementation freeze and synthetic checks only if the new design requires software beyond existing tools; no evidence-driven changes to the accepted ASF executable. Omit this step if existing tools suffice. |
| 3. Execute the fixed pilot and report observability | Research/evidence using the agreed tools | Retain every eligible opportunity and intended receiver, including nonresponse, inaccessible records and unknown dates. Apply the new feasibility rule once and publish its result before proposing a causal test. This is not primarily a writing-only or software task. |
| 4. Decide whether a new causal design is warranted | Writing/synthesis | If the pilot supports sufficient observability, preregister a new causal comparison with its own gates and coding/blinding plan. If it fails, report that limit and reassess domain priority without retroactively repairing this run. |

The first step must disclose exposure to the current result. It must fix intake
by sender actions and intended receivers before receiving outcomes are known,
so visible responders cannot become the selected denominator. It must keep a
known receiving act, nonresponse and an undocumented response distinct; retain
uncertain date intervals; and make shared-family aggregation explicit. A dated
sender assertion alone cannot establish the receiving action. Prospectively
stored public bytes can address version provenance only where capture actually
occurs; they cannot recover private acts or certify their historical dates.

The pilot needs its own prospectively declared observability benchmark and
resource ceiling. Neither resource exhaustion nor low observability may be
relabeled a successful test of a causal claim. It must be executable by one
unfunded researcher with public sources and local tools; optional replication,
private access, paid archival help or external contacts cannot be completion
gates. Its review must establish a viable coding-context separation if a later
causal design reaches delayed blinded recoding. This proposal does not authorize
source collection, start a new washout or add a new schedule.

The deferred print/Reformation follow-up retains its accepted alternative
discriminator: original dated authority/negotiation evidence and linked outcomes,
with the unfinished historical sampling obligations explicit. It is not
reopened here. Owner review of the cycle synthesis can change the next priority
without altering the accepted ASF stop.
