## Findings That Change the Programme

### Formal possibility is not structural generality

The [robustness report](../../models/antinomy_robustness/README.md) preserves
equilibrium, asymmetric lock-in, and operational collapse across the tested
variants under the frozen five-cell/four-seed survival criterion. Period-two
oscillation is absent from every sampled sequential trajectory. The two
asymmetric synchronous arctangent scenarios contain some oscillatory seed
runs but fail the survival criterion: failed survival is not proof of
mathematical nonexistence. The 5,166 uncoupled control trajectories produce
neither lock-in nor oscillation within this sampled model.

Consequently, oscillation must be presented as schedule- and response-dependent,
not as a generic signature of antinomy. Operational collapse is also sensitive
to the chosen capacity floor in the reference diagnostics; it is not a
measurement of institutional catastrophe. The tested perturbations do not
cover random schedules, all initial conditions, or common-shock models.
Empirical work must establish its own mechanisms, timing, and viability
observations rather than transfer attractor labels onto institutional histories.

### Institutional selection has evidence; topological priority does not

The historical pass contributes 19 dated observations and their source audit.
Its [ledger](../cases/print-reformation-ledger.md) supports three bounded
consequences, with the limits retained from the accepted interpretation:

1. Saxon authority and personnel precede the production and targeting of the
   1528 printed instruction. The ducal comparison connects wider reform to
   the 1539 succession while preserving the earlier Freiberg exception.
   Instructional production does not establish observed use, comprehension,
   uniform compliance, or a standardization feedback effect.
2. Venetian dissent, public authorization, censorship orders, and enforcement
   are distinct observations. Their separation supports investigating
   bargaining and selective institutional control, not a single censorship
   switch or a blanket absence of Protestant activity.
3. On the audited dates, a magistracy established on 22 April 1547 cannot be
   triggered by the result of Mühlberg on 24 April. This rejects only that
   direct-trigger reading. Earlier wartime or diplomatic pressure remains
   possible, and the cited study's later firm-level estimates are unreplicated,
   not refuted by this chronology check.

The five-domain reconstruction has not outperformed the stronger reduced
accounts already specified in the design. State-plus-grievance and
topology-plus-demand explanations must remain genuine rivals. More domain
labels are not an explanatory gain unless an additional evidenced link
accounts for an observation a reduced account misses.

The accepted stopping point is a first evidence pass, not a completed historical
test. The eight-stratum bibliographic sample, full durability/non-adoption
outcomes, and comparable topology measures remain incomplete. Missing entries
remain unknown, not zero. The catalog audit selects the present method; it does
not establish that network construction is impossible.

### A solo study can have consequential claim decisions

The ASF registration is accepted design infrastructure, with no case evidence
collected. Its original anchor is
`00d696ed80bd388955f622e0611853165f19508c`; the accepted, dated pre-evidence
amendments remain part of the operative packet. The
[manifest](../cases/asf-autonomy-coordination-registration.toml) and
[codebook](../cases/asf-autonomy-coordination-codebook.md) control execution,
not this summary.

The independent, unfunded programme must be completable without outside human
aid, paid access, or institutional affiliation. The amended study therefore
uses frozen first-pass records, delayed blinded recoding, and an executable
integrity audit. This tests intra-rater stability, not independent reliability.
Optional external replication cannot become a completion requirement.

The same screened corpus supports reciprocal-effect tests, nested
decision-authority tests, and reusable coverage, episode, and missingness
records. Deterministic sampling limits routine local-release coding to at most
48 families; it does not cap eligible cross-boundary records. A failed gate
produces a reported failure at that gate, not permission to replace the cohort,
discard unknowns, or keep recoding until a desired result appears.

## Ordered Handoff for the Next Cycle

Propose an ASF-centered next cycle, rather than expand every open line at once.
This priority is a research judgment, not a measured cost estimate: the accepted
design already fixes rival-discriminating outcomes and several bounded claim
consequences from one reusable corpus. No claim upgrade is promised. The next
cycle's scope and activation still require an explicit decision.

1. **Implement and freeze the ASF audit before retrieval.** Deliver blank
   schemas, deterministic sampling, date/horizon logic, G1–G3 and nested-claim
   calculators, blinded-recode generation, and synthetic tests against the
   accepted packet. Preserve its registration anchor and amendments. Review
   this as a separate item; do not mix implementation with outcome exposure.
2. **Execute the registered access/cohort gate, then the evidence comparison
   only if its prerequisites hold.** Publish coverage and failure information
   if G1 fails. Otherwise preserve the entire required screening frame,
   cross-boundary census, local baseline sample, shocks, and rival matrices.
   Do not narrow the corpus after seeing whether the cases are informative.
3. **Complete the solo audit and claim decisions.** Lock and hash the first
   pass, observe at least 14 complete calendar days without ASF evidence work,
   then recode and apply the registered initial-agreement and integrity rules.
   Time since preregistration is not this washout. Report primary and both
   sensitivity decisions, including negative, unstable, or indeterminate
   results; do not silently turn an incomplete run into a completed test.

The next historical follow-up has lower priority than the ASF sequence but a
clear discriminator: seek the original 22 April act and preceding negotiations,
visitation returns linked to the Saxon instruction, and dated Venetian
authorization or exception evidence. Use only accessible public material and
record unavailable sources; completion cannot depend on paid archival help.
An existing supplement and a small edition crosswalk should be feasibility
checks before a larger catalog extraction, not assumed ready-made data.
This is a proposed separate packet, not work started by the synthesis.

## Conclusions and Next Steps

This record warrants closing the second cycle on review and acceptance. The
cycle narrowed CM-04's formal scope, replaced empty historical evidence slots
with bounded institutional sequences, and fixed an independently executable
test with meaningful claim consequences. It did not demonstrate a universal
recurrence law, establish topological priority, or raise any claim above C1.

The programme now needs discriminating observations more than further generic
illustrations. After acceptance, the next proposed item is the ASF executable
audit, followed by the registered evidence and solo-stability sequence under
separate authorization. The historical comparison's missing sample and outcomes
remain explicit obligations; cycle closure does not discharge them.
