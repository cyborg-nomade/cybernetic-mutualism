through a predetermined favorable finding:

1. Identify the accepted executable/protocol versions and retain all exposure
   disclosures and amendments. Report whether the required corpus is complete.
2. Apply G1, G2, and G3 in order. If a gate fails, state the failed gate and
   publish the available descriptive records and missingness. Subsequent causal

   Resource exhaustion or unfinished collection alone is an incomplete run,
   not a successful completion or evidence against a claim.
3. Report the 90-day decision and independent 60/180-day sensitivities whenever
   those analyses are reached. Preserve all opportunity denominators and the
   distinct-family sign rules. Identify timing sensitivity at either horizon.
4. Give CM-01, CM-12, and CM-13 only their registered, conditional ASF-domain C2
   routes. Otherwise retain C1 and state the bounded negative, narrowing, or
   indeterminate implication. Give CM-04 its retain/narrow/withhold
   observational-mapping decision while retaining the numerical model's C1
   structural narrowing. No outcome validates toy parameters or attractors.
5. State what changes in the wider programme and the next discriminating
   action. No manifesto revision or confidence increase is required to close.

with public sources and local tools. External recoding is optional future
replication. The delayed recode measures intra-rater stability, not independent
reliability. Its washout starts at the first-pass lock, never preregistration.
