## Purpose and Programme Decision

Move the public argument forward while testing whether the empirical programme
can obtain the observations its next causal comparison would require. The third
cycle passed source access and executable integrity but stopped at G2: jointly
classifiable receiver/timing records covered 0/34 A → C and 1/41 C → A families
at every registered horizon. G3 and causal comparisons were unreached. All
claims remain C1; CM-04's observational mapping is narrowed for that design.
Those accepted results remain unchanged.

The proposed cycle has two independently consequential outputs. The pilot asks
whether a new, fixed intake can yield dated receiving acts across its eligible
receiver set. The essays develop the project's argument through primary-text
criticism and a precise account of communication's material constraints. Blog
writing is a required strand with its own acceptance criteria, not a summary
post to be attempted only after an empirical success.

Select the next two unwritten entries in the Roadmap's provisional sequence:
“Marx against the *Philosophy of Misery*” and “the base has a topology.” The
repository already contains the first two essays in that sequence. Choosing
these two advances the existing publication architecture without treating the
unfinished print/Reformation comparison as a completed historical case.
Their final arguments and titles remain revisable after source work.

## Editorial Deliverables

### Item 1: Marx against the Philosophy of Misery

**Roadmap connection:** Publication entry 3; thesis modules 1, 2 and 8; principally
the conceptual foundations of CM-01 and CM-02 and the later communication-turn
argument. This is a paired close reading, not a new empirical coupling test.

The essay should reconstruct a bounded set of Marx's economic and dialectical
objections to Proudhon's 1846 text and examine the relevant Proudhon passages.
It must distinguish an objection supported by the text, a disagreement about
the explanatory method and the project's own subsequent reconstruction.
Identify the edition/translation and exact passage for each central attribution;
check quoted or paraphrased Proudhon against Proudhon rather than relying only
on Marx's presentation. Later Proudhonian work may clarify a development but
cannot silently replace the 1846 object of criticism.

Choose and defend a focused thesis after the source record is complete. Assess
the strongest objection on its own terms, state which parts survive the
comparison and what they require the project to change or qualify. Avoid a compulsory verdict that Marx either
wins everything or merely misunderstands everything. The source record should
make clear which works or sections were read, which were consulted selectively
and which remain unchecked. Relevant bibliography can be created within the
item; the repository's planned bibliography is not assumed to exist already.

The accepted deliverable is a full essay and its bounded research support,
not an outline, list of quotes or promise of later close reading. Explain what
is inherited and what is reconstructed in terms a reader can follow without
having read the internal claim registry.

### Item 5: The Base Has a Topology

**Roadmap connection:** Publication entry 4 and thesis module 3; principally
CM-07, CM-08 and the layer distinctions bearing on CM-10. The essay should make
a defensible argument about material constraints while keeping causal priority
conditional and comparative.

Distinguish physical substrate, technical capacities, possible connections,
protocol/ownership/routing authority and observed use. Declare what a node and
edge mean in the worked illustration. A small explanatory graph or causal
diagram should clarify a mechanism; it need not become a new simulation or an
unmeasured historical network presented as data.

Give a difficult case or rival sequence in which ownership, productive
organization or political authority shapes communication arrangements. The
accepted print/Reformation first pass can support only its existing bounded
process observations; its unfinished sample and topology remain unfinished.
The ASF G2 stop may illustrate the difference between a documented channel and
an observed effect, but supplies no measured transmission graph or evidence
for topological priority. These limits must remain visible if either accepted
case is used. Any new historical or technical example needs its own checked
sources and bounded interpretation during the essay item.

The post must state the observation that would favor a rival ordering or
weaken the argument. Do not treat nominally new media, public posting, formal
jurisdiction or the absence of a dated receiving act as sufficient causal
proof. No universal communication-first hierarchy or automatic confidence
increase is a prerequisite for a publishable essay.

### Review, style and publication boundary

Both essays follow [the blog style guide](../../docs/STYLE.md): the project's
ironic, politically explicit voice, ordinary lowercase sentence openings,
American spelling, connected prose and no blog tables. Primary quotations,
proper names, titles and identifiers retain their faithful forms. The research

Style must not strengthen a claim beyond its sources.

Each essay needs stable draft front matter under [the post format](../../posts/README.md),
inline primary/authoritative source links, and a clear distinction between
factual support, intellectual influence and an illustrative analogy. Check
attributions and quotations directly, inspect the generated HTML/local preview,
and run the publisher's credential-free dry run. Link relevant accepted work
without making the blog dependent on internal repository navigation.

Completion means two owner-reviewed, publishable local drafts with the source
record and corrections needed to support them. This is substantive blog-writing
progress even if live release is deferred. WordPress draft creation, live
publication and social distribution are separate actions under the
[publishing runbook](../../docs/PUBLISHING.md). This plan does not authorize
those external writes. Publication can follow review when the owner requests
it; a publication count is not evidence for the underlying claims.

## Observability Pilot: Design Commitments

This cycle plan is not the pilot's preregistration. Item 2 must choose and
freeze the following before new prospective case evidence is inspected:

- A manageable domain, sender-action intake rule, opportunity-family unit and
  complete intended-receiver universe, with the rationale and prior exposure
  disclosed. Candidate scope may reuse the ASF setting, but it is a new future
  intake, not a relabeling of the accepted 2023–2025 corpus.
- Finite intake dates and follow-up endpoint, capture cadence, local-work budget
  and deterministic volume handling. Do not extend intake or follow-up until
  enough convenient responses appear. Any calendar schedule must begin after
  design/tool acceptance; no prospective window is backdated by this plan.
- Separate rules for occurrence, authorized actor, public availability,
  receiving conduct and temporal order, retaining intervals and explicit
  unknowns. Capture immutable bytes, original identifiers, retrieval times,
  version histories and hashes where public access permits.
- The joint classifiability measure, family/project denominators, shared-family
  aggregation and minimum intake required to interpret a fraction. Intake is
  defined by sender actions and intended receivers, never by visible receiving
  outcomes. A nonresponse, an unknown response and a dated unchanged disposition
  must remain distinguishable.
- Feasibility thresholds, integrity checks and decisions for sufficient
  observability, low observability, insufficient intake, access failure and
  budget/incomplete-work stops. Zero eligible opportunities yield no measured
  classifiability fraction. Resource exhaustion cannot manufacture a measured
  failure or a completed collection result beyond the registered stop's scope.
- A solo interpretation/verification plan and any coding-context separation it
  actually requires. External collaborators, paid services, private access or
  contacts with participants cannot be primary completion gates. Automation is
  optional and requires its actual scope and notifications to be specified.

The prior G1–G3 thresholds and frozen implementation remain attached to the
prior study. Reusing a rule in the pilot requires an explicit prospective
choice and rationale; a new rule cannot retroactively repair the old result.
The pilot's benchmark is observational feasibility, not a disguised test that
a reciprocal or one-way mechanism exists. A positive pilot result would warrant
considering a new causal preregistration. A negative or insufficient result
would warrant a design/domain decision, with missingness published. Neither
outcome alone raises CM-01, CM-04, CM-12 or CM-13 above C1.

If new software is needed, item 3 keeps it in a separate versioned location
and tests retrieval/provenance, date intervals, family/receiver membership,
unknown handling and the pilot's actual arithmetic against synthetic inputs.
Otherwise record why the existing tools are sufficient and skip a software
build. No new model sweep is required merely to give the cycle a code item.
The pilot report must preserve its initial accepted submission and any later
corrections; a later causal study needs a separately accepted design.

## Completion and Decision Criteria

The cycle can close when the following are accepted:

1. Both full Roadmap essays, with checked central attributions, explicit rivals,
   rendered previews and local draft metadata. An empirical stop does not waive
   this writing obligation. Unavailable primary evidence narrows an attribution
   or thesis; it must not be replaced with an invented close reading.
2. The prospective pilot registration and either a justified existing-tool
   disposition or the required frozen new implementation, before intake.
3. The report for the pilot path actually reached under its finite registered
   design, including unavailable sources, unknown dates, all eligible receivers,
   insufficient intake and any integrity/budget stop. An unfinished run must
   remain explicitly incomplete unless a prespecified stop concludes its scope.
4. A bounded claim/revision record separating textual corrections, conceptual
   clarifications, observation feasibility and causal evidence. Retain current
   confidence unless a distinct accepted evidential rule warrants a change.
5. A synthesis explaining what the two essays changed in the public argument,
   what the pilot changed in measurement feasibility, what remains unresolved
   and the next discriminating action. Owner acceptance closes the cycle.

A feasible pilot is not a condition for the writing strand to succeed, and two
accepted essays do not replace the registered pilot report. Avoid expanding
the cycle into the full print/Reformation comparison, a new causal coupling
study, hub-formation or substrate-transition simulations, or the entire
manifesto revision queue. Those remain possible later items with their own
scope. Unexpected pilot findings are recorded as exploratory, not inserted
into an essay as a preregistered causal confirmation.
