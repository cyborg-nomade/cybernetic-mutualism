# First Research Cycle Synthesis and Decision Record

- **Decision date:** 2026-09-01
- **Status:** Accepted for the first-cycle close, subject to repository review
- **Scope:** The glossary, claim registry, *After Dark Mutualism*, the
  print/Reformation paired design, and the minimal two-variable antinomy model
- **Claims directly affected:** [CM-04](../claims.md#cm-04--cybernetic-dynamics-can-formalise-antinomies)
  and [CM-05](../claims.md#cm-05--social-orders-can-regenerate-antagonism)

## Decision

The first research cycle meets its declared completion condition. It produced
all five planned artifacts and exposed a necessary revision rather than merely
completing a checklist: the universal assertion in manifesto section 2 that
every order generates new asymmetries and counterforces is broader than the
available argument or evidence can support. The manifesto now states CM-05 as
a bounded, falsifiable tendency and carries a dated revision note.

This close does not validate the wider theory. No claim rises above C1. The
cycle improved definitions, separated kinds of claim, demonstrated limited
formal possibilities, and specified empirical tests; it did not supply
historical evidence for a general recurrence law or for the causal priority of
communication topology.

## Results

1. The [glossary](../glossary.md) established provisional operational
   distinctions among antinomy, topology, substrate, centralisation,
   decentralisation, control, complexity, and progress. This is conceptual
   infrastructure, not empirical confirmation.
2. The [claim registry](../claims.md) separated 24 load-bearing claims by type,
   scope, rivals, failure conditions, confidence, and dependency. Its main
   result is auditability: failure of one claim no longer automatically counts
   as failure of the whole programme.
3. [*After Dark Mutualism*](../../posts/after-dark-mutualism.md) corrected the
   earlier portrayal of Proudhonian progress as linear and established a
   sharper question for chronological source work. It did not complete that
   source history.
4. The [print/Reformation paired design](../cases/print-reformation-paired-design.md)
   converted CM-07 through CM-09 into rival sequences and decision rules. It
   produced no case finding because the evidence pass has not begun.
5. The [minimal antinomy model](../../models/antinomy/README.md) distinguished
   equilibrium, asymmetric lock-in, period-two oscillation, and an operational
   low-viability state. A sampled uncoupled ablation removed lock-in and coupled
   oscillation. These results provisionally retain CM-04 within the model's
   declared formal domain only.

## Warranted Revision

The first-cycle artifacts do not warrant the universal proposition that
**every** social order generates new asymmetries and counterforces. The
proposition ranges over unspecified orders, mechanisms, scales, and durations;
it also cannot distinguish endogenous recurrence from external shocks or from
the trivial observation that societies continue to change. The model does not
rescue the universal: it imposes a two-variable coupling and demonstrates
possible regimes, not a general law of institutional history.

CM-05 is therefore retained at C1 in bounded form: durable social orders may
tend to generate new asymmetries, conflicts, and counterforces when
differentiated positions accumulate advantages, environments change, or
controls provoke organised responses. Long-duration orders that remove a
specified asymmetry without functional replacement and matched cases in which
external shocks explain recurrence better remain admissible counterevidence.

The revision changes the [manifesto](../../MANIFESTO.md#2-antinomies-do-not-end)
from certainty to a testable historical hypothesis. It does not revise the
more limited conceptual claim in CM-01 that **some** opposed tendencies can be
co-constitutive, nor does it claim that stable low-conflict orders have already
been observed.

## Unresolved Claims

- **CM-01:** No empirical antinomy has yet shown directed dependence in both
  directions against common-cause and uncoupled rivals.
- **CM-02:** The chronological Proudhon source study remains incomplete.
- **CM-04:** Robustness to asymmetric coupling, asynchronous updates, and an
  alternative bounded response function remains untested.
- **CM-05:** Its domain, observation window, asymmetry measures, and comparison
  class still require preregistration.
- **CM-07 through CM-09:** The print/Reformation design has not assembled or
  coded evidence, so topology-first, state-first, production-first, and
  reciprocal accounts remain open.
- **CM-10, CM-20, and CM-22:** The registry has identified category or scope
  problems in substrate sequence, historical direction, and metaphysical
  determinism. They remain in the revision queue because this cycle did not
  adjudicate replacement language for them.

## Negative and Limiting Findings

- The model's reciprocal enablement and inhibition are not separately
  identifiable; only their net cross-effect is varied.
- Fifteen parameter cells remain unresolved, 73 are seed-sensitive, and the
  published robustness checks concern selected examples rather than the whole
  parameter map.
- The model assumes symmetry, synchronous updating, a sigmoid response, and an
  imposed antinomic relation. It contains no actors, institutions, networks, or
  measured historical quantities.
- The uncoupled ablation is informative only inside the sampled toy model. A
  common-shock rival has not yet been implemented or compared.
- The paired historical case is a preregistered design, not evidence for print
  causation. Its catalogs may support only bounded process traces rather than a
  commensurable network dataset.
- The cycle found no basis for raising the confidence of CM-04, CM-05, or the
  communication claims above C1.

## Consequences for the Research Programme

The cycle leaves the programme viable but more conditional. Antinomy must be
shown through discriminating reciprocal effects rather than inferred from the
presence of opposition. Formal regime distinctions are worth testing, but they
cannot establish historical recurrence. Communication topology remains a
candidate constraint whose priority must be compared against state,
production, demand, and common causes case by case.

The proposed second cycle should therefore join three lines of work: stress the
toy model's restrictive assumptions, freeze an empirical antinomy design before
collecting observations, and begin the first evidence pass for the
print/Reformation comparison. The public essay should explain the conceptual
gain and the model's limits without presenting either as an empirical result.

## Conclusions and Next Steps

This record warrants closing the first research cycle because its five
artifacts produced a concrete correction: CM-05 and manifesto section 2 now
state a bounded tendency rather than a universal law. The change leaves CM-04
provisionally retained at C1, leaves the historical and communication claims
unconfirmed, and makes the programme's main negative findings part of its
baseline rather than exceptions to be forgotten.

The next discriminating action is to review and accept the bounded manifesto
revision as the second cycle's entry gate. After that decision, model
robustness work and the print/Reformation evidence pass can proceed
independently; the empirical antinomy preregistration must be frozen before its
evidence is collected and should incorporate the robustness results before its
model comparison is finalised.
