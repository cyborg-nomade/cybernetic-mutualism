# Print/Reformation Source and Catalog Audit

## Access and Provenance

Retrieval date: 2026-09-08. Coding freeze: `fdea00e`, committed before this
pass's retrieval. Read this audit with the [codebook](print-reformation-codebook.md),
[ledger](print-reformation-ledger.md), and [first-pass analysis](print-reformation-evidence-pass.md).
The records below describe material actually inspected. Search snippets only
identified candidates; failed opens were retried through public HTTP retrieval
or an alternative source. No private archive, paid service, or outside reviewer
was used. Modern summaries are distinguished from edited primary texts.

URLs and locators support reinspection, but the release contains original notes,
not full copyrighted source copies or an immutable snapshot of every webpage.
Unless noted otherwise, reuse rights were not established beyond public reading;
no permission to redistribute a catalog or scan is implied. All translations
and paraphrases in the ledger are this project's, not independent witnesses.

For a future crosswalk, retain `catalog`, `record_id`, `edition_group_id`,
`copy_id`, original and normalized title, author, printer/publisher (distinct
roles), place as stated, place attribution, date interval, language, format,
holdings, and source URL. Preserve both uncertain and false-imprint attributions.
The title-page/colophon distinction in EDIT16 is not automatically a
publisher/printer distinction. A library shelfmark identifies a copy; a VD16 or
CNCE identifier identifies a bibliographic record. Merge across catalogs only
after matching edition statements and checking variants. No cross-catalog merge
was executed in this pass.

S12's Data availability statement points to online supplementary material. The
article's exposed links resolve to a supplementary PDF; this pass did not inspect
that file or establish an executable raw-data package. Its promising reuse route
must therefore remain open, not be described as unavailable. Even a verified
firm-output panel would not by itself supply reception or transmission edges.

The intended eight-stratum sample is **incomplete**. The freeze's ranking cannot
be applied globally without enumerated search results. The following are audit
candidates, not a representative sample or claimed earliest editions. The first
pass's process-tracing stop rule permits this documented shortfall; no output
frequency or network metric is calculated from it.

These searches preferentially surfaced known documents and accessible copies.
They cannot estimate survival, confessional composition, or the first appearance
of a genre. Catholic control and ordinary liturgy are represented among inspected
texts/metadata, but an ordinary-devotional and Catholic-answer scan sample remains
unfinished. This restriction prevents treating a Protestant selection as the
regional printing industry.

## Conclusions and Next Steps

Choose **bounded process tracing for this release**. Public records support an
auditable chronology, and individual digital objects are technically retrievable;
the regional universe, matching rules in operation, and meaningful transmission
edges have not been demonstrated. The audit also identifies concrete hazards:
composition dates mistaken for edition dates, liturgical origin mistaken for
printing place, and multiple descriptions mistaken for independent editions.

This redirects CM-07–CM-09 toward explicitly sourced event comparisons without
claiming that a network study is impossible. The next economical data action is
to inspect S12's supplement, test a bounded EDIT16 extraction, and reconcile a
small set of editions with VD16/USTC before extending the corpus. No external
assistance is required to complete the present bounded result.
