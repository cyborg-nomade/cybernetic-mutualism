# after dark mutualism

Names can hide a change of subject. This one should make the change explicit.

In 2020 I published an essay called ["dark mutualism, part
1"](https://antinomiaimediata.wordpress.com/2020/02/05/dark-mutualism-part-1/).
It tried to recover mutualism as a theory of historical development rather than
only a shelf of economic proposals. It argued that progress was nonlinear, that
decentralisation could produce new concentrations of power, and that opinion
and communication networks mattered to political organisation. Those questions
still organise this project.

The answers no longer do.

The old essay joined those questions to a neoreactionary account of power. It
treated egalitarian humanism as the hidden creed of a globally dominant
opinion-forming apparatus; borrowed Moldbug's "Cathedral" as if it named a
coherent causal system; and imagined that mutualism could become realistic by
aligning itself with impersonal forces hostile to equality and perhaps to
humanity itself. [A related
post](https://antinomiaimediata.wordpress.com/2019/09/11/if-nature-is-unjust-were-all-screwed/)
went further and treated reality as if it were the final measure of justice.

That was not innocent provocation around an unchanged core. It was a political
and analytical mistake. *Cybernetic mutualism* replaces the name because the
project now rejects that mistake while retaining the problems it obscured.

## what the darkness was trying to name

"Dark" first named a refusal. It refused three comforting pictures of social
change:

1. that history is a moral ascent;
2. that a sufficiently correct ideal can be installed by collective will; and
3. that decentralisation, equality, or revolution can end the production of
   power and conflict.

Each refusal contains a problem worth keeping. Institutions do not become
possible because their advocates are right. Formal equality can coexist with
concentrated control over infrastructure, attention, and decision. A network
can distribute its edges while centralising discovery or routing. A revolution
can abolish one ruling class and produce another. Good intentions do not
provide requisite variety, accurate signals, or a working correction channel.

The darkness also registered a real dissatisfaction with political humanism
when humanism means an uncaused, transparent subject standing outside the
systems that formed it. People act through bodies, institutions, inherited
categories, material incentives, and partial models of their situations.
Collective subjects are no more sovereign than individual ones. "The people,"
"the market," "the party," and "humanity" do not acquire a single mind merely
because a theory gives them a singular noun.

But a refusal is not yet a theory. The adjective made obscurity sound like
depth. Worse, it allowed several distinct claims to slide into one another:

- that moral wishes do not suspend causal constraints;
- that liberal institutions can conceal concentrated power;
- that human beings are not metaphysically sovereign;
- that equality is therefore false or destructive; and
- that reactionary descriptions of power are therefore privileged guides to
  reality.

The first three do not entail the last two. The old project crossed that gap by
posture rather than argument.

## a correction about progress

The 2020 essay said that Proudhon's rationalism expected a linear progression
from A to B. That was too easy, and it was poorly sourced.

In the 1853 [*Philosophy of
Progress*](https://theanarchistlibrary.org/library/pierre-joseph-proudhon-the-philosophy-of-progress),
Proudhon does not define progress as a guaranteed improvement or a march toward
a final social form. He opposes progress to the absolute: movement,
transformation, and susceptibility to revision against whatever presents itself
as fixed, complete, or definitive. The text does include material and moral
improvement among the products of progress, but it refuses to make that
restricted sense the whole philosophy.

This does not prove the present project. The available English text is a
working translation, Proudhon's uses change across works, and cybernetic terms
such as feedback, phase transition, and path dependence are later
reconstructions. The project's [claim
registry](https://github.com/cyborg-nomade/cybernetic-mutualism/blob/main/research/claims.md#cm-02--mutualism-as-a-theory-of-progress)
therefore keeps the historical claim at low confidence until a proper
chronological close reading is complete.

It does establish something narrower: movement without a fixed final form does
not need to be imported into mutualism from the political right. The old essay
mistook a source it had not adequately read, then recruited that mistake into a
story of reactionary correction.

What remains is a more disciplined hypothesis. Mutualism may contain resources
for thinking progress as serial and conflictual transformation without a
guaranteed terminus. Progress in this project is never an unqualified good. It
must be progress in a named capacity, for a named population or system, at a
declared scale, across a stated interval, with losses elsewhere kept visible.

## what remains mutualist

The project remains mutualist for reasons deeper than mutual credit or a
preference for small institutions.

First, it begins from antinomy. In the 1846 [*System of Economic
Contradictions*](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/444), Proudhon distinguishes
an antinomy from a logical error and describes opposed terms as necessary to
one another. His formulations are not identical across the book, and some do
move toward a third term. The inheritance is therefore a question, not a
scripture: when do opposed tendencies reproduce one another, and what kinds of
dynamics follow?

Liberty and authority, competition and cooperation, centralisation and
decentralisation may form such relations under specified conditions. They need
not be equally desirable or equally strong. They need not reach equilibrium.
They may stabilise, oscillate, lock in, bifurcate, or collapse. Calling a pair
an antinomy is justified only when the mechanism of mutual production can be
shown.

Second, the project retains the idea of collective force. Organised relations
produce capacities that cannot be assigned to isolated participants. But the
collective is not thereby harmonious, conscious, or morally superior. Its
additional capacity can be appropriated unevenly. The organisation that
produces power also creates positions from which some actors can redirect it.

Third, it retains federation as a problem of organisation across scales. A
federation is not decentralisation purified of authority. It is an attempt to
make local autonomy and common coordination correct one another without a final
guarantee against capture. Whether a federation succeeds is an empirical
question about information, enforcement, exit, memory, resources, and the
distribution of decision rights.

Finally, it retains development through encounter. A theory changes when it
meets resistant sources, difficult cases, rival mechanisms, failed models, and
criticism. The correction of the old name is itself meant to follow that rule.

## what is left in the dark

Four parts of the previous project are not coming along.

### the Cathedral as a master explanation

The old essay noticed that power can be distributed across universities, media,
bureaucracies, firms, and professional networks. It then borrowed a polemical
category that gathered those heterogeneous institutions into one ideological
actor.

That move solved the research problem by naming it. It supplied no stable rule
for identifying nodes or edges, no measure of centralisation, no account of
conflict within the alleged system, no comparison with ownership and state
power, and no observation that could show the category to be wrong. Agreement
among selected institutions became evidence of coordination; disagreement
could become evidence of adaptive complexity. A concept able to absorb both
outcomes explains neither.

Cybernetic mutualism keeps communication networks as an object and discards the
master category. A defensible analysis must separate infrastructure, protocol,
ownership, routing authority, observed use, and message content. It must ask
which actors can address whom, through which intermediaries, at what cost, with
what capacity for reply. It must compare the network hypothesis with class,
production, state, demographic, ecological, and common-cause explanations.

### reality as justice

Description is not endorsement. A hierarchy can recur without being rightful.
A system can persist by making its costs invisible or by exporting them to
people excluded from its feedback channels. An institution can be stable,
adaptive, and cruel. Domination does not acquire moral authority by surviving.

The old appeal to reality as the measure of justice collapsed the distinction
between a system's capacity to reproduce itself and the reasons anyone should
accept it. It also made criticism incoherent: injustice could only mean
something insufficiently real, while every durable victor certified itself.

The new project makes the opposite commitment. Recurrence, fitness, stability,
and control are descriptive variables. Normative judgements require their own
premises, affected standpoints, and arguments. No model output can silently
provide them.

### alignment with impersonal forces

To explain a tendency is not to receive an instruction from it. Capital
accumulates; states concentrate means of coercion; platforms exploit network
effects; arms races select for escalation. None of these processes becomes a
political subject whose direction should be followed merely because it is
powerful.

The accelerationist hope that history's impersonal machinery could be pushed to
its extremity repeated the teleology it claimed to escape. It imagined that the
process would perform the political choice and eventually deliver a rupture.
Cybernetic mutualism instead asks conditional questions: under these feedbacks,
constraints, and delays, which regimes become more likely? Which interventions
alter the range? Who bears their errors and costs? There is no guarantee that
intensification selects anything emancipatory.

### antihumanism as an enemy list

There is a methodological reason to remove the sovereign human subject from the
centre of social explanation. There is no corresponding reason to treat living
persons as noise, obsolete equipment, or obstacles to a more advanced synthetic
order.

Nor can "egalitarian humanism" serve as a single causal variable. Equality
before law, equal moral worth, universal franchise, administrative
standardisation, redistribution, and professional management are different
institutions and commitments. They can reinforce or oppose one another. Their
effects must be separately specified. Bundling them into a secular creed
reproduces the same idealism the old project accused in its enemies.

The present project is post-humanist only in this limited methodological sense:
human intention is not the sole cause, scale, or measure of social processes.
It is not anti-human in its politics.

## why cybernetic

Cybernetics is not a brighter mood word. It names obligations the old adjective
did not impose.

Norbert Wiener's 1948 title joined [control and communication in the animal and
the machine](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL43167544W/cybernetics).
W. Ross Ashby's [*An Introduction to
Cybernetics*](https://ashby.info/Ashby-Introduction-to-Cybernetics.pdf) gave
formal accounts of transformation, feedback, regulation, and requisite
variety. These formalisms do not determine which variables ought to be
controlled, who may choose their viable ranges, or who should bear the costs of
regulation. That political incompleteness is a warning against treating
cybernetics as a source of legitimacy.

Here *cybernetic* means that an explanation should, where appropriate, name:

- the system boundary and observation scale;
- the variables and viable ranges at stake;
- the channels through which signals and actions travel;
- the positive and negative feedbacks, delays, thresholds, and saturation;
- the disturbances, resource and energy constraints, and unmodelled shocks;
- the actors who set targets, alter regulators, manipulate reports, or bear
  error; and
- the observations that would favour a rival explanation.

It also means that control has no position outside the controlled system.
Parties, states, markets, sciences, media, and models observe partially and act
with delay. Their interventions change the systems and sometimes the signals
they use to judge success. Every controller can become a disturbance; every
decentralisation can generate hubs; every correction mechanism can be gamed.

That is a research programme, not a dark revelation.

## continuity and rupture

| Dark mutualism | Cybernetic mutualism |
| --- | --- |
| Progress is nonlinear and may include reversal. | Direction is disaggregated by capacity, scale, interval, and cost; no general tendency is assumed before evidence. |
| Decentralisation can produce new centres. | Recapture is a conditional hypothesis involving scale economies, preferential attachment, interoperability, and exit cost. |
| Opinion networks organise power. | Communication topology is represented in declared layers and tested against production-, state-, class-, and common-cause rivals. |
| Subjects are produced by impersonal forces. | Agency is causally embedded, but description does not erase political responsibility or moral standing. |
| Egalitarian humanism is the hidden enemy. | Equality, franchise, management, standardisation, and moral worth are distinct claims and institutions requiring separate analysis. |
| Reality supplies justice; history's forces should be followed. | Stability and recurrence confer no legitimacy; causal tendencies generate conditional predictions, not commands. |

The continuity is real. So is the rupture. The project still doubts final
syntheses, sovereign subjects, frictionless decentralisation, and politics by
wish. It no longer treats reaction as a privileged outside, naturalises winners,
or makes hostility to equality the price of materialism.

## an accountable change of name

The old essay will remain public. A theory committed to correction should not
manufacture a past in which every revision was already implicit. The point is
not to claim that "dark" always meant what "cybernetic" means now. It did not.

The record matters because the failure is instructive. The old project had
identified some genuine problems: the reproduction of hierarchy, the capture
of decentralised systems, the material power of communication networks, and the
limits of political voluntarism. It weakened those insights by placing them
inside a totalising story that selected its causes through an enemy image and
protected itself from contrary evidence.

Cybernetic mutualism begins after that darkness by making the questions harder.
It will have to define its terms, specify mechanisms, compare rival histories,
build models that can fail, and revise claims in public. It may discover that
communication topology is less causally prior than proposed, that its preferred
five-domain circuit earns none of its complexity, or that some alleged
antinomies are ordinary conflicts with no mutual production at all.

If the new name means anything, it means those results must be allowed to
count.
